Excited to share **CareCircle — Connected Elderly Care**, our hackathon build for the Elderly Care & Medication Management challenge.

Managing medications and doctor's appointments can be overwhelming, especially when loved ones live in different places. We designed CareCircle to bring elderly individuals, family, and caregivers into one secure, shared care space.

What we built:
- Scheduled medication tracking, dose confirmations and adherence insights
- One-time invitations with owner/family/caregiver/viewer permissions
- Smart email reminders and **unconfirmed-dose** caregiver check-ins
- Doctor appointments with 24-hour and 1-hour reminders
- Large-text support, spoken daily briefings and a 7-day progress dashboard
- An ownership-transfer flow to keep older adults in control of their care

Tech: **FastAPI, JavaScript, Google Cloud Run, Firestore, Cloud Scheduler** and optional SendGrid.

Our aim: help people stay independent while making care more collaborative and transparent.
